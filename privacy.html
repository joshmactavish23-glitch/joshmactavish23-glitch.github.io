Blackbeard Kids Uploader - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how "Blackbeard Kids Uploader" (the "App"), run by Blackbeard Kids, handles information.

Who uses the App

The App is a private tool used only by Blackbeard Kids to upload its own children's videos to its own TikTok account. It has no public users.

Information we use

The App only uses the access TikTok grants for the Blackbeard Kids account (such as a login token and basic account info) to post videos. It does not collect personal information from any other person, including viewers or children.

How information is used

This access is used only to upload videos to the Blackbeard Kids account. We do not sell, rent or share any data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Storage and security

Login tokens are stored privately and securely and are used only by Blackbeard Kids. You can remove the App's access at any time in TikTok's settings.

Children's privacy

The App does not collect any information from children. Videos are made for children, but the App only posts content and does not gather viewer data.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The latest version will always be on this page.
